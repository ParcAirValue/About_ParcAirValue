# Communal

### A little help, already on the way.

**The best delivery route may be one someone’s taking anyway.**

Communal connects people who need something delivered with neighbors already traveling that way. One shared trip can make getting an item across town a little more human.

[Visit Communal](https://communal.nandan.fyi/) · [View the GitHub repository](https://github.com/ParcAirValue/About_ParcAirValue) · [Read how it works](https://communal.nandan.fyi/guidelines)

> **Deliveries are free during launch. No payment is collected.**

---

## Which sounds like you?

<details>
<summary>📦 I need something delivered</summary>

A box of books needs a new home. Post what needs to go and where. A neighbor heading that way can offer to bring it along—you decide whether to accept.

1. **Post a request** — Share what needs to go, its destination, and when it should arrive.
2. **Choose an offer** — Review offers and decide who you’re comfortable coordinating with.
3. **Confirm delivery** — Arrange the handoff privately and confirm when your item arrives.

[Post a request →](https://communal.nandan.fyi/post)

</details>

<details>
<summary>🚲 I’m already traveling</summary>

You’re heading across town already. Share your route and when you’re going. If a request fits your trip, you can offer to carry it and coordinate directly.

1. **Share your trip** — Let neighbors know your general route and travel time.
2. **Find a request** — See whether someone needs an item carried along your way.
3. **Coordinate** — Choose whether to help and agree on the handoff together.

[Share your trip →](https://communal.nandan.fyi/trips/new)

</details>

## A practical kind of community

Not another delivery fleet. Just a place for people with a need to meet people with a route—and work out the handoff together.

- **People set the plan.** The requester and traveler coordinate each handoff.
- **Privacy comes first.** Exact addresses aren’t shown on the public board.
- **Free during launch.** There’s no payment processing or charge to use Communal.

Communal is a community marketplace, not a courier service. Learn about handoffs, item restrictions, and what the service does—and doesn’t—provide.

[Read the community guidelines →](https://communal.nandan.fyi/guidelines)
